the staging area is empty. add a starter from quicklaunch, or click a glyph in the reservoir to forge one. hover a placed widget to see its tooltip + composed URI. each row shows its width budget; every widget occupies exactly its declared width.

forge — drafts

⚠ unplaced drafts in the forge — place them or they won't be in the export.

no drafts. click a glyph in the reservoir to start one, ctrl+click to stack several onto one draft, or pick a ramp for a gauge.

rows — budget & separators

each row has a budget in cells and a default separator printed between widgets (none at the edges). a widget can override the one in front of it with sep.

manifest — no-data behaviour

a widget with no data shows the placeholder (an animated "collecting data" glyph), or blank cells when hidden. either way it keeps its width.

actions — wire ONE per card

click to assign to the active (highlighted) draft. actions are templates over sensor readings ({sensor.reading}); picking one binds the sensor it reads. they stay templates in the export — the statusline fills them at render time.

quicklaunch — add a starter

prebuilt widgets, sensors + width + tooltip + action prewired. lands straight on the last row (if it fits the budget).
